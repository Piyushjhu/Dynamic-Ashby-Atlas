What the axes measure

HEL and
spall strength

HEL is the longitudinal stress at the elastic precursor in a shock experiment. Spall strength is the tensile failure stress inferred from the release-wave history.

The map compares the onset of inelastic response with resistance to tensile failure. Test method, loading rate, pulse duration and material state affect both quantities and should guide comparisons.

Material clouds

What a cloud represents

Each cloud summarises the available HEL and spall-strength extents for a named material across studies or conditions. Some bounds use cross-paper matches or a HEL estimate, identified in the selection details. Use clouds to assess literature coverage; use the measured-pair view for coordinates reported from the same test. Cloud shape and area have no statistical interpretation.
