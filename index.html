A clearer comparison

Two properties.
A broader perspective.

The Hugoniot elastic limit describes the onset of inelastic response under shock loading. Spall strength describes resistance to tensile failure during release.

Reading them together reveals where exact materials sit relative to one another, while the underlying test conditions and evidence remain one click away.

A necessary distinction

Coverage is not confidence.

Clouds enclose pooled property extents for a named material. Some use cross-paper matches or a conditional HEL estimate. They are schematic coverage regions, not measured covariance or statistical confidence intervals.
