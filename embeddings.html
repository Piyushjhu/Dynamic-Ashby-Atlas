Reading the geometry

A cluster starts a question.
It does not answer it.

01

Separate inputs from outcomes.

If HEL or spall is an input, separation coloured by that same property is expected. Such views describe the outcome space and cannot show which static parameters control shock response.

02

Check the paper effect.

Repeated shots from one paper share material, apparatus and reduction choices. Nearby points may reflect a study rather than a general material law. Inspect source-neighbour diagnostics and paper counts alongside class colours.

03

Ask what is missing.

A complete-case cohort can be small and selective. Missing static properties are not filled by class averages. An empty analysis is retained with its reason instead of inventing a multivariable relationship.

Exploration, not a causal or predictive result

UMAP emphasises local neighbourhoods; diffusion coordinates describe connectivity through a kernel graph. Neither method establishes that a parameter causes a change in spall strength or HEL. Global cluster gaps, axis directions and cluster areas require particular caution.

Changing input features, scaling, neighbours or random seed can change the picture. The downloadable payload records the feature set, cohort, method and available stability diagnostics so that the plotted view can be reviewed.
