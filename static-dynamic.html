Why the axes cannot be converted directly

Yield strength describes the onset of plastic flow in a specified loading test; HEL describes the longitudinal elastic precursor under shock. Fracture toughness quantifies crack resistance for a specific method and geometry; spall strength describes dynamic tensile failure under a particular pulse and reduction method. A ranking on one map need not survive on the other.

Without matched material states across all four measured properties, this compilation cannot establish a material-by-material rank change or calibrate a static-to-dynamic prediction.
