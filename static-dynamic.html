Interpreting the comparison

What the two maps
can show

Why the axes cannot be converted directly

Yield strength describes the onset of plastic flow in a specified test. The Hugoniot elastic limit (HEL) is the longitudinal stress at the elastic limit under shock loading. Fracture toughness measures crack resistance for a specified method and specimen geometry; spall strength measures resistance to dynamic tensile failure for a particular pulse and analysis method.

The maps show which property ranges and material classes the available literature covers. Comparing the static and dynamic performance of a particular material requires comparable material states and test conditions.
