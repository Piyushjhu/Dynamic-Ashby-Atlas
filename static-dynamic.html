Methods, assumptions and limits

These are associations within a heterogeneous literature compilation. A class changing rank does not establish a causal effect, a matched-material change, or a prediction of dynamic properties from static tests. Method choices, source coverage and test conditions can change the ordering.

Why the axes cannot be converted directly

Yield strength describes the onset of plastic flow in a specified loading test; HEL describes the longitudinal elastic precursor under shock. Fracture toughness quantifies crack resistance for a specific method and geometry; spall strength describes dynamic tensile failure under a particular pulse and reduction method.

Independent material sets are useful for comparing the coverage and class structure of these two maps. A claim that one particular material changes rank between regimes still needs comparable material states and test conditions; the clouds alone do not establish that relationship.
